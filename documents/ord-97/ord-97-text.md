ORDINANCE No. 97.

AN ORDINANCE changing the names of Turner, Pine, Georgia, Elm, Pacific and Rose
streets.

The Mayor and Council of the City of Los Angeles do ordain as follows:

SECTION 1. That the name of that street in said City of Los Angeles, in front of
the Pico House, extending from Main street to New High street, and heretofore
known as Turner street, is hereby changed, and the same shall be hereafter
designated and known as Sonora street.

SEC. 2. That the name of that street in said City of Los Angeles, in Bell's
Addition and the Los Angeles Homestead Tract, heretofore known as Pacific street,
is hereby changed, and the same shall be hereafter designated and known as
Montgomery street.

SEC. 3. That the name of that street in said City of Los Angeles, between Bell's
Addition and the Los Angeles Homestead Tract, heretofore known as Pine street, is
hereby changed, and the same shall be hereafter known and designated as Cherry
street.

SEC. 4. That the name of that street in said City of Los Angeles, in Bell's
Addition, heretofore known as Georgia street, is hereby changed, and the same
shall be hereafter known and designated as Georgia Bell street.

SEC. 5. That the name of that street in said City of Los Angeles, in the Los
Angeles Homestead Tract, heretofore known as Elm street, is hereby changed, and
the same shall be hereafter known and designated as Bush street.

[p. 294]

SEC. 6. That the name of that street in East Los Angeles, in said city,
heretofore known as Rose street, is hereby changed, and the same shall be
hereafter known and designated as Kuhrts street.

SEC. 7. All ordinances and parts of ordinances in conflict with this ordinance
are hereby repealed.

SEC. 8. The Clerk of the Council shall certify to the passage of this ordinance,
and cause the same to be published once in the Los Angeles Daily HERALD, and
thereafter it shall take effect and be in force.

I hereby certify that the foregoing ordinance was adopted by the Council of the
City of Los Angeles, at its meeting of September 1st. A. D. 1883.

                                        W. W. ROBINSON,
                    Clerk of the Council of the City of Los Angeles.

Approved this 3d day of September, A. D. 1883.

                                        C. E. THOM,
                                                    Mayor.

Published September 11th, 1883.

## Reading notes (not part of the text)

- **Sec. 1's full stop after "September 1st"** is the volume's; it is a printer's
  slip for a comma and is transcribed as printed.
- **Six sections, six streets, five different locatives.** Only Sec. 1 gives a
  from-to extent. Secs. 2-5 identify the street by the TRACT it lies in or
  between, and Sec. 6 only by the district ("East Los Angeles"). The locatives
  are disambiguators — there were several Turners, several Roses, at least three
  Elms and two Georgias in the city — not stretches carved out of a longer
  street.
- **Bell's Addition and the Los Angeles Homestead Tract are adjacent**: Pine runs
  BETWEEN them (Sec. 3) while Pacific runs through both (Sec. 2), Georgia is in
  Bell's alone (Sec. 4) and Elm in the Homestead Tract alone (Sec. 5).
