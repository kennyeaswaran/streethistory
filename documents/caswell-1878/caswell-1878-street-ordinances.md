# Caswell 1878 — the street shortlist

Every entry in *Revised Charter and Compiled Ordinances and Resolutions of the
City of Los Angeles* (Wm. M. Caswell, comp., 1878) that **names, renames,
vacates, opens, extends, widens, grades or defines the lines of a street.**

Read off the volume's own *Index to Ordinances and Resolutions*, printed
pp. 525–549, transcribed and eye-checked in
[`caswell-1878-contents.md`](./caswell-1878-contents.md), which also holds the
PDF↔folio offset table and the full text of every renaming.

⚠ **This volume has no ordinance numbers.** Los Angeles did not number its
ordinances until No. 104 of November 1883. Caswell numbers **sections**, §1 to
§736, printed in the outer margin, across ordinances and resolutions alike.
Cite as **Caswell 1878, §N (p. N)**. Do not invent ordinance numbers.

**PDF page** is the page in `caswell-1878.pdf` (552 leaves, three Gale citation
leaves inserted). `printed ≤200 → PDF = printed` · `printed 201–400 → PDF =
printed + 1` · `printed 401–549 → PDF = printed + 2`.

**Nothing here has been entered as a row.** This is a shortlist for extraction
later, per the brief.
