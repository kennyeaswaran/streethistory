# Teed 1889, Vol. IV — the street shortlist

Everything in this volume's **LIST OF ORDINANCES** (Nos. 318–432, June 1887 –
Jan. 1889) that names, renames, vacates, opens, extends, widens or defines the
lines of a street. Read against `teed-1889-contents.md`, which carries the
verified list and the PDF↔folio offset table.

**PDF page = printed page + 4** for Ords. 318–410 (printed 81–196) and
**+ 5** for Ords. 411–432 (printed 197–232). The shift is a Gale citation leaf
at PDF 201.

⭐⭐⭐ = a renaming instrument · ⭐⭐ = states an extent this project can use ·
⭐ = worth a look · "routine" = no street-history content beyond the name.

---

## 4. What is NOT here

- **No omnibus renaming.** Nothing titled "changing the names of certain
  streets" exists anywhere in Nos. 318–432. Teed 1887 (Vol. III) has three of
  them — Ords. 207, 242 and 314 — and Vol. IV has none.
- **No Lemon, Wilson, Charity, Texas, Belmont-as-name, High, Short, Georgia
  (bare), Alabama, Virginia, Fort, Pearl, Court or Teed renaming.** Every one
  of those names that appears at all appears in a grading or vacating context.
  What the volume gives on those names is extents and dates of survival, listed
  in FINDINGS.md §3.
