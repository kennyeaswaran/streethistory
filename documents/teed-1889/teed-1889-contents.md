# Teed 1889, Vol. IV — contents transcribed

*Charter and Compiled Ordinances and Resolutions of the City of Los Angeles*,
Vol. IV, compiled and indexed by **Freeman G. Teed, Clerk of the Council**
(Los Angeles: Evening Express Company, 1889). Gale, *The Making of Modern Law:
Primary Sources*, doc id `DT0106634468`.
Local scan: `documents/teed-1889/teed-1889-vol4.pdf`, 744 PDF pages, **no text layer**.

Transcribed 2026-09-19 by a Claude instance: OCR first (tesseract, 200 dpi),
then every line below checked by eye against a 300 dpi render of the page.
Where the OCR and the eye disagreed, the eye won and the OCR reading is not
recorded. Where the **book itself** is wrong, the printed reading is kept and
the error is flagged `⚠`.

(4); Ord. 345 on p. 117 at PDF 121 (4); Ord. 411 on p. 197 at PDF 202 (5);

**What the volume covers.** Ordinance No. 318 was adopted **June 20, 1887**;
Ordinance No. 432 was adopted **December 7, 1888** and approved January 3,
1889, with a certificate of January 7, 1889 on the following leaf. The
resolutions run to No. 1303, into January 1889. So the volume is the record of
roughly **June 1887 – January 1889**, picking up where Teed 1887 (Vol. III,
Ords. 114–317) stops.

## 3. LIST OF RESOLUTIONS — described, not transcribed

PDF 238–249 = list pages i–xii, covering **Resolutions Nos. 543–1303**. It is a
by-number list of one-line titles in four repeating forms:

- *"Intention to improve / grade / pave / sidewalk / sewer a portion of X street"*
- *"To change and establish the grade of X street"*
- *"Ordering X street improved / graded"*
- *"Award of contract to grade / pave / sewer X street"*

⚠ **Not transcribed here, deliberately.** Every one of the ~760 entries is
reachable under its street's name in the INDEX below, which is transcribed in
full and is the better finding aid for this project (it is alphabetical by
street and it states extents). The only resolutions in the list that are *not*
grade/contract business are noted in `teed-1889-street-ordinances.md` §3.

Sampled and verified against the page images: the list's first entry is
Res. 543 (p. 233) and its last is Res. 1303, "Award of contract to grade Patton
street" (p. 707).

## 5. Errors and oddities in the book itself

These are the book's, not the transcriber's. They are listed so nobody
"corrects" a reading that is right.

1. **The LIST OF ORDINANCES omits No. 369.** It jumps 368 → 370. Ordinance 369,
   "An Ordinance vacating a portion of Soto street," is printed in the body at
   p. 149 (PDF 153) and *is* indexed ("Soto street, portion of vacated … 369 …
   149").
2. **The LIST OF ORDINANCES gives Ord. 370 the page "140".** The body prints
   Ord. 370 on p. 149, immediately after 369. 140 is a transposition of 149.
3. **The index enters Ord. 368 twice, under two different streets.** Under
   *Belmont avenue*: "certain portions of vacated … 368 … 148" — correct. Under
   *McFarland, A.*: "deed to, for that part of **Beaudry avenue** vacated … 368
   … 148" — wrong; the ordinance's own text says Belmont avenue throughout.
4. **Spelling slips in the index, printed as they stand:** "East **Eegeware**
