ORDINANCE No. 200.

An Ordinance changing the name of Wilhart street to Hayes street, and of Sumner
street to Walnut street.

The Mayor and Council of the City of Los Angeles, do ordain as follows:

Section 1. That the name of Wilhart street, in the Pioneer Building Association
tract, be and the same is hereby changed to Hayes street.

Sec. 2. That the name of Sumner street, in the Pioneer Building Association
tract, be and the same is hereby changed to Walnut street.

Sec. 3. The Clerk of the Council shall certify to the passage of this
ordinance, and shall cause the same to be published once in the Los Angeles
Daily HERALD, and thereupon and thereafter it shall take effect and be in force.

I hereby certify that the foregoing ordinance was adopted by the Council of the
City of Los Angeles, at its meeting of November 10, 1885.

W. W. ROBINSON,
Clerk of the Council of the City of Los Angeles.

Approved this 16th day of November, 1885.

E. F. SPENCE,
Mayor.

Published November 17, 1885.

## Notes on the reading

- Both changes are qualified by a **tract** — "in the Pioneer Building
  Association tract" — and not by cross streets.
- The spelling is **Wilhart**, twice (title and Section 1), and the volume's
  LIST OF ORDINANCES and GENERAL INDEX both repeat it.

## Where these streets were, from this volume's own pages

- **Res. 408** (p. 495) — "to establish grade of **Hayes street, from Hoff to
  Kuhrts street**". **Res. 415** (p. 502) — "to establish grade of **Walnut
  street, from Hoff street to Kuhrts street**". The two new names are graded
  between the same pair of cross streets, which is what two parallel streets in
  one tract should look like.
- The GENERAL INDEX adds for **Walnut street**: grade at Hoff street, at Kuhrts
  street, at Chavez street, from Hoff street to Kuhrts street, "name of Sumner
  street changed to … 200". For **Hayes street** (printed p. 672) the same Hoff /
  Kuhrts pair.
- Hoff, Kuhrts and Chavez streets put the Pioneer Building Association tract on
  the east side of the river, in the Aliso / Boyle Heights approach — the same
  ground Res. 398, 403, 424, 535 and 534 work over.
