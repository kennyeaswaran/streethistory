# One sheet, two filings — this is the BOTTOM half

`MR005-307.pdf` (M.R. 5-307) carries **two unrelated recorded maps on one sheet
of paper**, stacked vertically. They were surveyed by the same engineer two
months apart and recorded for different people, so they are two documents, not
two halves of one: two titles, two recording dates, two pieces of ground.

The model allows one alignment and one coverage polygon per document
(MODEL-SPEC §4.4, §4.6), which is the same reason a multi-page scan becomes one
document per page. So the render was cut in two.

- **`mr005-307-a`** — the top map:
  *Map of Block W Mott Tract as subdivided by E. T. Wright at request of
  M. L. Wicks, June 1883, in Los Angeles City.* Scale 60 ft = 1 in.
  Recorded at the request of M. I. Wicks, June 25, 1883.
  Bounded by Flower Street (N), Pearl Street (S), First Street (W),
  Second Street (E). Lots 1-16.
- **`mr005-307-b`** (this folder) — the bottom map: Hamilton's Subdivision of part of the
  Urquidez Garden (Fifth St / San Pedro St), recorded Sept. 12, 1883.

## How the cut was made

`<id>-100dpi.png` here is the full-sheet 100 dpi render cropped to rows
**1020-1758** of 1759 (full width, 1142 px). Pixel y=0 in this folder is the
full sheet's y=1020. The cut line is the midpoint of the
widest blank horizontal band on the sheet (rows 940-1099, found by row ink
profile, not by eye). Both halves stay at 100 dpi, so the project's alignment
convention still holds.

`mr005-307-b.pdf` is the unaltered county scan of the whole sheet, copied in
rather than cropped — the same arrangement as the per-page folders under
`mr030-009-p*`, where each folder holds the full multi-page PDF and only the
render is one sheet. Cite the PDF's URL, read the render.
