# Teed 1887, Vol. III — the street shortlist

Everything in this volume's **LIST OF ORDINANCES** (Nos. 114–317, December 1883 –
June 1887) that names, renames, vacates, opens, extends, widens or defines the
lines of a street, plus the resolutions that state a usable extent. Read against
`teed-1887-contents.md`, which carries the verified lists and the PDF↔folio
offset table.

**PDF page = printed page + 6** for Ords. 114–~250 (printed 5–194) and
**+ 7** for Ords. ~251–317 (printed 195–293). The shift is a Gale citation leaf
at PDF 201. Resolutions: **+ 17** to printed 384, **+ 18** to 584, **+ 19**
thereafter (citation leaves at PDF 402 and 603).

⭐⭐⭐ = a renaming instrument · ⭐⭐ = states an extent this project can use ·
⭐ = worth a look · "routine" = no street-history content beyond the name.

⚠ **All numbers on this page are OLD SERIES.** See `FINDINGS.md` §9.
