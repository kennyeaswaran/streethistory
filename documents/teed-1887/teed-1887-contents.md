# Teed 1887, Vol. III — contents transcribed

*Compiled Ordinances and Resolutions of the City of Los Angeles*, Vol. III,
compiled and indexed by **Freeman G. Teed, Clerk of the Council**
(Los Angeles: E. H. Freeman, Book and Job Printer, 1887). Gale, *The Making of
Modern Law: Primary Sources*, doc id `DT0106633769`.
Local scan: `documents/teed-1887/teed-1887-vol3.pdf`, 703 PDF pages,
**no text layer**.

Transcribed 2026-09-19 by a Claude instance: OCR first (tesseract, 200 dpi),
then every line of the LIST OF ORDINANCES, the LIST OF RESOLUTIONS and the
GENERAL INDEX read by eye against a contrast-normalised render of the page.
Where the OCR and the eye disagreed, the eye won and the OCR reading is not
recorded. Where the **book itself** is wrong, the printed reading is kept and
the error is flagged `⚠`.

⚠ **Scan quality.** Roughly one leaf in twenty is a grey, low-contrast image
(the Gale capture varies). Those pages return nothing from plain OCR and were
re-processed (`convert -normalize -level 30%,78%`) before reading; they are
legible by eye. The pages that were blank even then are listed in §5.

**What the volume covers.** Ordinance No. 114 was adopted **December 24, 1883**
and approved January 5, 1884; Ordinance No. 317 was adopted **June 13, 1887**
and approved June 17, 1887. The resolutions run from No. 222 (adopted
January 1884) to No. 542. So the volume is the record of roughly
**December 1883 – June 1887**. It picks up where Robinson 1884 stops and is
picked up in turn by Teed 1889, Vol. IV (Ords. 318–432, from June 20, 1887).

⚠⚠ **SERIES.** These are **OLD SERIES** ordinance numbers — the run the city
began numbering retrospectively in November 1883. The 1889 charter restarted
the numbering at 1 (New Series), so `documents/ord-286` in this corpus is
**Ordinance No. 286 of the old series** and is *not* the same instrument as any
New Series No. 286. Every document folder created from this volume says so in
its `title`. See FINDINGS.md §9 for the collisions checked.
