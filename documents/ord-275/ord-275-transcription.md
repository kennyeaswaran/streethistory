# Ordinance No. 275 (Old Series) — full text

Transcribed by eye, 2026-09-19, from a contrast-normalised 200 dpi render of
**PDF page 240** of `documents/teed-1887/teed-1887-vol3.pdf` = printed page
**233** of *Compiled Ordinances and Resolutions of the City of Los Angeles*,

---

ORDINANCE No. 275.

An Ordinance changing the name of Texas street to Belmont avenue.

The Mayor and Council of the City of Los Angeles, do ordain as follows:

Section 1. That the name of that certain street in the City of Los Angeles,
herefore known as Texas street, be changed, and that hereupon and hereafter it
shall be known and designated as Belmont avenue.

Sec. 2. All ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict with this ordinance
are hereby repealed.

Sec. 3. The Clerk of the Council shall certify to the passage of this
ordinance, and shall cause the same to be published once in the TRIBUNE, and
thereupon and thereafter it shall take effect and be in force.

I hereby certify that the foregoing ordinance was adopted by the Council of the
City of Los Angeles, at its meeting of January 3, 1887.

FREEMAN G. TEED,
Clerk of the Council of the City of Los Angeles.

Approved this 6th day of January, 1887.

W. H. WORKMAN,
Mayor.

Published January 7, 1887.

---

## Notes on the reading

- **"herefore"** in Section 1 is the book's slip for "heretofore"; the parallel
  clause in Ordinance No. 286 reads "heretofore". Kept as printed.
- **No extent clause.** The name is changed, entire.
- Section 3 names the paper simply as "the TRIBUNE", not "the Los Angeles Daily
  Tribune" as the surrounding ordinances do.

## Where Texas Street ran, from this volume's own pages

⚠ **The GENERAL INDEX gets this wrong and the resolutions get it right** — see
`documents/teed-1887/FINDINGS.md` §4. The index page 679 prints a block of grade
entries under the heading "Texas street" that belong to **Tenth street**, and a
block under "Tenth street" that belongs to **Texas street**. Checked against the
resolutions themselves:

- **Res. 305** (p. 389) — "to establish the grade of **Tenth street**, from Hill
  street to Main street" — indexed under *Texas*. Wrong.
- **Res. 491** (p. 582) — "to establish the grade of **Texas street**, from
  **Temple street to Diamond street**" — indexed under *Tenth*. Wrong.
- **Res. 509** (p. 599) — "to change and establish the grade of **Texas street**,
  from **Temple street to Court House street**" — indexed under *Tenth*. Wrong.
- **Res. 482** (p. 573) — "to establish the grade of Temple street, from **Texas
  street** to a point 2900 feet west of Texas street".
- **Res. 380** (p. 468) — "to establish the grade of Temple street, from
  Centennial avenue to a point 100 feet west of the west line of **Texas
  street**".

So **Texas Street ran north–south from Court House street through Temple street
to Diamond street**, on Crown Hill west of downtown — which is where
`texas-st` and `belmont-ave` already sit, and where the "Map of Los Angeles
Railway and Railroad circa 1889" letters the Second Street Cable Railroad's
western terminus "TEXAS ST. (BELMONT AVE.)".
