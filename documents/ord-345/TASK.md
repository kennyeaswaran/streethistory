# TASK — ord-345 carries no row, and here is exactly why

## What is missing

Nothing about the ordinance. The text is complete, the number is now known, the
approval and publication dates are known. **What is missing is a decision only
Kenny can make**, and until it is made a row here would double-count one act.

## The situation

`documents/ord-1887-08-22` and `documents/ord-345` are **the same ordinance**:

| | ord-1887-08-22 | ord-345 |
|---|---|---|
| what it is | the City Clerk's **minute-ledger copy**, ledger pp. 700–701 | the **official printed copy**, Teed Vol. IV p. 117 |
| ordinance number | "Ordinance No. —" (blank) | **345** |
| adopted | Aug. 22, 1887 | Aug. 22, 1887 |
| approved | not recorded | Aug. 30, 1887, L. N. Breed, Mayor pro tem. |
| published / in force | required; date unknown | Sept. 2, 1887 |
| Sec. 1 wording | "…known as **Virgin**…" | "…known as **Virgin street**…" |
| rows | `change virgin → alpine` (whole-name), plus a `state` row for the extent | **none** |

`ord-1887-08-22` also carries something this document cannot: a `state` row
putting Virgin Street between Yale Street and Beaudry Avenue, read from a
**grading resolution in the same council session**, which is not part of the
ordinance and is not reprinted in Teed.

## The decision

Three options, in what I take to be descending order of tidiness:

1. **Leave the row where it is and treat `ord-345` as corroboration.** This is
   the precedent already in the corpus — `ord-1613` carries the Scott Avenue
   rows and `lah-1893-04-04`, the council report of the same meeting, carries
   none. Then `ord-1887-08-22`'s title and header should be edited to say
   "Ordinance No. 345" and to cite this folder, and the WANTED entry for the
   number closes.
2. **Move the change row here** and leave `ord-1887-08-22` holding only the
   `state` row with the extent. Defensible on the ground that the printed text
   is the authoritative one and the ledger is a copy — but it splits one
   session's evidence across two folders for no gain.
3. **Merge the two folders.** Not recommended: they are two artifacts with two
   provenances, and §4.4a's instinct is that the artifact is the unit.

⚠ **An instance may not do any of these**, because 1 and 2 both require editing
`documents/ord-1887-08-22/`, which this pass was told to stay out of, and
because "which document is the instrument of record" is a judgement, not a
transcription.
