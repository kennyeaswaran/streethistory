# Robinson 1884 — the street shortlist

Every ordinance and resolution in *Compiled Ordinances and Resolutions of the
City of Los Angeles* (W. W. Robinson, comp., Marley & Freeman, 1884) that
**names, renames, vacates, opens, extends, widens or defines the lines of** a
street. Grade and improvement resolutions are not street acts in that sense and
are listed separately at the end, because their cross streets are the most
useful thing in the book.

**PDF page = printed page for pp. 3–200; PDF page = printed page + 1 from
printed p. 201 onward.** Gale inserts its own citation leaf at PDF p. 201. Full
note in `robinson-1884-contents.md`.

**Titles are as printed.** The volume sets "AN ORDINANCE" / "A RESOLUTION" in
small capitals; that is rendered here as ordinary capitals on the first two
words and normal case after, which is the only change made. Every title in the
first two tables below was checked against the page image.

## 3. Where the cross streets are — grade and improvement resolutions, by street

Not street acts in the sense above, but this is what the volume is mostly made
of and it is the most useful thing in it: **every one of these names a street
together with the streets it crosses**, which is exactly what this project needs
