# ord-242 — what is missing

**No rows are written on this document, and that is deliberate.**

## 1. Section 1 already has its row elsewhere

`documents/lah-1886-07-23/` carries `guadalupe → davies-2nd`, `scope: "extent"`,
2nd Street from Alameda Street to Garey Street, and its note already names
"Ordinance No. 242". Duplicating it here would pin one transition twice.

⭐⭐⭐ **THAT ROW'S OPEN QUESTION IS NOW ANSWERED.** Its note says *"“Geary” is
read as GAREY … A spelling inference all the same; Kenny to confirm."*

Two things from this volume:

1. **The city's own printed ordinance also spells it "Geary"**, so the
   mis-spelling is the council's, not the Herald compositor's.
2. **Resolution No. 508** (printed p. 598, PDF 617, adopted March 7, 1887 — eight
   months after the ordinance) establishes the grade of *"Guadalupe street, from
   Alameda street to **Garey** street"*, and spells **Garey** six times on the
   page. Same street, same two ends. It also names two intermediate crosses the
   ordinance leaves out: **Rose street** and **Hewitt street**, both live
   entities (`rose-street`, `hewitt`).

So the reading is right, and `lah-1886-07-23`'s row could drop its "Kenny to
confirm" and cite Res. 508 instead. That is a one-line edit to another agent's
document, so it is recorded here rather than made.

⚠ Worth noticing separately: **Res. 508 still calls the street GUADALUPE in March
1887**, eight months after Ord. 242 renamed it. See `FINDINGS.md` §7 on names
that outlive or precede their ordinances in this volume.

## 2. Section 2 has no row and cannot have one

> "The name of that certain street, extending from **Temple street to Bellevue
> avenue**, and known as **Cemetery avenue**, be and the same is hereby changed
> to **Castelar street**."

Blocked on **one missing name entity**: `cemetery-avenue`. `castelar` exists and
is lettered in the corpus; `bellevue-ave` exists; Temple Street exists. The
moment a Cemetery Avenue entity exists, this is a `scope: "extent"` row with both
ends named — one of the cleanest qualified changes in the volume.

⚠ `names.js` is the user's to edit (CLAUDE.md, hard rule). The recommendation,
with the supporting resolutions, is in `documents/teed-1887/FINDINGS.md` §5.

**Supporting evidence already in hand**, from this volume's GENERAL INDEX and
LIST OF RESOLUTIONS:

- Res. 425 — "Cemetery avenue, grade of, from Sand street" (p. 516)
- Res. 311 — "Accepting grading of Sand street, from Cemetery avenue" (p. 395)
- Res. 312 — "Ordering payment for grading Sand street, from Cemetery" (p. 396)
- Ord. 242 itself — Temple street to Bellevue avenue

So Cemetery Avenue ran from Temple Street past Sand Street to Bellevue Avenue,
in the Fort Hill / old Catholic cemetery ground north-west of the Plaza — which
is where modern Castelar Street is.
