ORDINANCE No. 242.

An Ordinance changing the names of certain streets.

The Mayor and Council of the City of Los Angeles, do ordain as follows.

Section 1. The name of that certain street, extending from Alameda street to
Geary street, and known as Guadalupe street, be and the same is hereby changed
to Davies street.

Sec. 2. The name of that certain street, extending from Temple street to
Bellevue avenue, and known as Cemetery avenue, be and the same is hereby
changed to Castelar street.

Sec. 3. The Clerk of the Council shall certify to the passage of this ordi
nance, and cause the same to be published once in the Los Angeles Daily HERALD,
and thereupon and thereafter it shall take effect and be in force.

I hereby certify that the forgoing ordinance was adopted by the Council of the
City of Los Angeles, at its meeting of July 19, 1886.

W. W. ROBINSON,
Clerk of the Council of the City of Los Angeles.

Approved this 22d day of July, 1886.

E. F. SPENCE,
Mayor.

Published July 23, 1886.

## Notes on the reading

- "do ordain as follows." ends with a full stop rather than a colon; "ordi
  nance" is broken without a hyphen at the line end; "forgoing" for
  "foregoing". All three are the book's, and all three are kept.
- ⚠ **"Geary street"** is what the volume prints, and it is what the Herald
  printed too (`documents/lah-1886-07-23/`). The corpus reads it as **Garey
  Street** — Ordinance No. 48 of 1889 continues Davies Street "from near Garey
  street eastward", which is the same junction. The spelling inference is
  recorded on the Herald document's row and is Kenny's to confirm; this volume
  does not settle it, because it repeats the same spelling.
- ⚠ The publication dates differ between the two copies: the Herald's version as
  transcribed carries no publication line, and this volume prints **July 23,
  1886** for Ord. 242 while printing **July 24, 1886** for Ord. 241 on the
  facing page — that is, the later ordinance published a day earlier. Probably a
  slip; recorded, not resolved.
- **Cemetery avenue → Castelar street** has no row anywhere in the corpus,
  because there is no `cemetery-avenue` entity. See `TASK.md`.
