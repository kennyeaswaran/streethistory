ORDINANCE No. 317.

An Ordinance changing the name of Deepwater street.

The Mayor and Council of the City of Los Angeles, do ordain as follows:

Section 1. That the name of that portion of Deepwater street, in the City of
Los Angeles, between Beaudry avenue and Philadelphia street, be changed to
Bartlett street.

Sec. 2. The Clerk of the Council shall certify to the passage of this
ordinance, and cause the same to be published once in the Los Angeles Daily
TRIBUNE, and thereupon and thereafter it shall take effect and be in force.

I hereby certify that the foregoing ordinance was adopted by the Council of the
City of Los Angeles at its meeting of June 13, 1887.

FREEMAN G. TEED,
Clerk of the Council of City of Los Angeles.

Approved this 17th day of June, 1887.

W. H. WORKMAN,
Mayor.

Published June 18, 1887.

## Notes on the reading

- ⭐ **A QUALIFIED change, and one of only three in the volume with a clean
  two-ended extent** (the others are Ord. 207 §1 and Ord. 242 §1). "That portion
  of Deepwater street … **between Beaudry avenue and Philadelphia street**" —
  both ends named, both streets live in the corpus.
- "Clerk of the Council of City of Los Angeles" drops the "the"; kept as printed.
- Ordinance No. 317 is the last in the volume. Teed 1889, Vol. IV picks up at
  No. 318, adopted a week later on June 20, 1887.

## Where Deepwater Street was, from this volume's own pages

- **Res. 334** (p. 420) — "Accepting grading of **Montreal street, from Sand and
  Deepwater streets**"; **Res. 336** (p. 422) — "Ordering payment for grading
  Montreal street, from Sand". So Deepwater met Montreal Street near Sand
  Street.
- **Res. 373** (p. 459) — "To establish grade of **Philadelphia street, from
  Bellevue avenue**", and the GENERAL INDEX's Philadelphia entry adds grade at
  Virgin street and at Bellevue avenue.
- So the renamed stretch lies in the Fort Moore / Bunker Hill ground between
  Beaudry Avenue and Philadelphia Street, among Montreal, Sand, Bellevue and
  Virgin — the same Beaudry hillside as Grasshopper Street (Ord. 232) and
  Bellevue Avenue (Ord. 152).
