## The big picture (context worth citing)

A commission spent six weeks revising street names citywide; its ordinance made
**over 350 changes**, against "barely a dozen protests." Committee's stated
findings: changes were made to remove duplicates or unify one name along a
street's length; over two-thirds of new names English, under one-third Spanish;
resulting city total ≈ 1,250 English vs. 150 Spanish street names (a
quantifiable anglicization moment — potentially map-worthy someday).

**⚠ THE FULL ORDINANCE (326/350+ changes) IS NOT IN THIS ARTICLE** — this is
only the committee's ~50 amendments to it. Finding the full text is still the
highest-value single research target for this project, but as of 2026-08 the
target has a name and an address: **Ordinance No. 4093 (New Series)**,
recorded in Ordinance Book IV at p. 337 (the Feb. 23 version) and p. 331 (the
Feb. 2 version the mayor returned unsigned). It was NOT printed in the Herald
(search record: omnibus-1897-renaming-full.md). See research-leads.md.
