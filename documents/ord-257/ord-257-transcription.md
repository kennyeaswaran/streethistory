ORDINANCE No. 257.

An Ordinance changing the name of High street.

The Mayor and Council of the City of Los Angeles, do ordain as follows:

Section 1. That the name of that certain street in the City of Los Angeles,
known as High street, be and the same is hereby changed to Walters street.

Sec. 2. The Clerk of the Council shall certify to the passage of this
ordinance, and cause the same to be published once in the Los Angeles Daily
HERALD, and thereupon and thereafter it shall take effect and be in force.

I hereby certify that the foregoing ordinance was adopted by the Council of the
City of Los Angeles, at its meeting of September 20, 1886.

W. W. ROBINSON,
Clerk of the Council of the City of Los Angeles.

Approved this 25th day of September, 1886.

E. F. SPENCE,
Mayor.

Published September 29, 1886.

## Notes on the reading

- **No extent clause.** Section 1 changes the name of the street, entire.
- The title names only the old street — "changing the name of High street" — and
  the volume's LIST OF ORDINANCES repeats it that way, which is why the entry
  was marked "contents unknown" before the page was pulled. The new name is in
  Section 1.
- ⚠ **High Street is not New High Street.** Both are live in this volume and both
  are indexed separately: New High street has its own grade resolutions (Res.
  471, from Virgin street to Temple street) and High street has only this
  renaming. Resolution No. 471 grades New High Street *past* Walters Street, so
  the two were neighbours, not the same street.

## Where it was, from this volume's own pages

- **Res. 471** (p. 563) establishes the grade of **New High street, from Virgin
  street to Temple street**, and lists its intersections in order: Virgin
  street — a point 177 feet south of Virgin — **Walters street** — **Bellevue
  avenue** — Marchessault street. So Walters (the former High) crossed New High
  Street between Virgin Street and Bellevue Avenue, in the Sonoratown grid north
  of the Plaza.
- **Res. 232, 246, 278, 279, 309, 310** all grade **Buena Vista street** "between
  High and Short" / "from Short street to High" / "between High and Virgin
  streets". So on Buena Vista the order was Short — High — Virgin, and Short
  became Bellevue avenue (Ord. 152 of this volume). The two readings agree: High
  Street lay between Short/Bellevue and Virgin.
- The GENERAL INDEX (printed p. 681) has the whole Walters entry, two lines:
  *"Walters street, grade of at New High street … 471 … 563"* and *"name of High
  street changed to … 257 … 197"*.
