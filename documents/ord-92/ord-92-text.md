ORDINANCE No. 92.

AN ORDINANCE fixing the name of Ducommun street.

The Mayor and Council of the City of Los Angeles do ordain as follows:

SECTION 1. That that street in the City of Los Angeles, extending from Alameda
street to Amelia street, and being the street south of Sainsevain street and the
next street thereto, and generally known as Ducommun street, but designated on a
map, recorded in book 2, pages 526 and 527, of miscellaneous records in the
office of the Recorder of Los Angeles county, as Commercial street, be and the
same is hereby named Ducommun street.

SEC. 2. The Clerk of the Council shall certify to the passage of this ordinance,

[p. 275]

and cause the same to be published once in the Los Angeles Daily HERALD, and
thereupon and thereafter it shall take effect and be in force.

I hereby certify that the foregoing ordinance was adopted by the Council of the
City of Los Angeles, at its meeting of July 28th, A. D. 1883.

                                        W. W. ROBINSON,
                    Clerk of the Council of the City of Los Angeles.

Approved this 30th day of July, A. D. 1883.

                                        C. E. THOM,
                                                    Mayor.

Published August 1st, 1883.

## Reading notes (not part of the text)

- **"book 2, pages 526 and 527, of miscellaneous records"** is M.R. 2-526/527,
  which this corpus holds as `documents/mr002-526-p1` and
  `documents/mr002-526-p2` — the Plan of the Subdivision of the Alanis Vineyard
  Tract, recorded May 5, 1869. Sheet 1 letters **Commercial Street** along what
  the corpus identifies as modern **Ducommun Street**.
- **Amelia street** is lettered on the same two sheets and is identified there
  with modern **Garey Street**.
- Two later resolutions in the same volume use the new name: Res. 162 (p. 271)
  establishes the grade of Ducommun street and Res. 164 (p. 272) is of intention
  to improve and grade it.
