ORDINANCE NO. 320.

An Ordinance changing the name and establishing the lines of Ogier lane.

The Mayor and Council of the City of Los Angeles do ordain as follows:

Section 1. That the lines of that certain street in the City of Los Angeles
heretofore known as Ogier lane are hereby declared to be laid down and defined
in a map entitled "Plat showing proposed re-location of Ogier lane, said lane
to be called and designated in the future Winston street," and filed with the
Clerk of the Council June 13, 1887; and further, that the name of said street
shall be changed to Winston street.

Sec. 2. That that portion of said Ogier lane not included in the lines
designated and laid down in said map is hereby vacated and abandoned.

Sec. 3. The Clerk of the Council shall certify to the passage of this ordinance,
and shall cause the same to be published once in the Los Angeles Daily Tribune,
and thereupon and thereafter it shall take effect and be in force.

I hereby certify that the foregoing ordinance was adopted by the Council of the
City of Los Angeles at its meeting of July 5, 1887.

FREEMAN G. TEED,
Clerk of the Council of the City of Los Angeles.

Approved this 7th day of July, 1887.

W. H. WORKMAN,
Mayor.

Published July 8, 1887.

## Notes on the reading

- The printed spelling is **Ogier**, twice in the ordinance and twice in the
  volume's index ("Ogier lane, lines of, established … 320 … 83"; "Winston
  street, name changed from Ogier lane … 320 … 83"), and once in the LIST OF
  ORDINANCES ("320.—Changing name of Ogier lane to Winston street"). ⚠ The
  1881 plat (`documents/mr005-110`) and the Herald of June 14, 1887
  (`documents/lah-1887-06-14`) both letter **Ozier**. Both spellings are real
  ink; this is the official one.
- "Sec. 2" vacates the part of the old lane left outside the new lines — so the
  ordinance is a re-location as well as a renaming, and the ground the name
  carries after July 1887 is *not exactly* the ground it carried before. The
  map that would say how much moved is "Plat showing proposed re-location of
  Ogier lane", filed with the Clerk June 13, 1887, and is not in this corpus.
- The same meeting is the one the volume's LIST OF ORDINANCES puts on printed
  p. 83; the preceding ordinance on the same page is No. 319 (Buena Vista-street
  bridge), adopted June 20 and approved June 30, 1887.
