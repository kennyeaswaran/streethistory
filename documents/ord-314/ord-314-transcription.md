ORDINANCE No. 314.

An Ordinance changing the names of certain streets.

The Mayor and Council of the City of Los Angeles, do ordain as follows:

Section 1. That that portion of that certain street known as Aliso avenue,
together with its extension between its intersection with First street and the
eastern boundary of the city, shall be hereafter known as First street.

That that portion of Pleasant street between Aliso street and the intersection
of First street and Aliso avenue shall hereafter be known as Aliso street.

That that certain street known as Boston street, in block 59 and block 60 of
Hancock survey, shall hereafter be known as Breed street.

That that certain street known of Breed street shall hereafter be known as Bird
street.

That that portion of the certain street known as Carlisle street, between Pine
street and Walnut avenue, shall hereafter be known as Los Angeles street.

That that portion of Los Angeles street, between Washington street and its
sounthern boundary, be hereafter known as Carlisle street.

Sec. 2. The Clerk of the Council shall certify to the passage of this
ordinance, and shall cause the same to be published once in the Los Angeles
Daily TRIBUNE, and thereupon and thereafter it shall take effect and be in
force.

I hereby certify that the forgoing ordinance was adopted by the Council of the
City of Los Angeles, at its meeting of May 16, 1887.

FREEMAN G. TEED,
Clerk of the Council of the City of Los Angeles.

Approved this 19th day of May, 1887.

W. H. WORKMAN,
Mayor.

Published May 25, 1887.

## Notes on the reading

- ⚠ **Six changes, all in Section 1, and only the first is numbered.** The five
  paragraphs after "Section 1" are unnumbered continuations of it, not separate
  sections; Sec. 2 is the publication clause. Quoted individually below as
  `sec-1-a` … `sec-1-f`.
- "known **of** Breed street" (fourth paragraph) is the book's error for "known
  **as** Breed street"; "**sounthern**" for "southern"; "**forgoing**" for
  "foregoing". All kept as printed.
- ⚠⚠ **Two of the six changes are a SWAP, and the order matters.** The fifth
  paragraph makes part of Carlisle Street into Los Angeles Street; the sixth
  makes part of Los Angeles Street into Carlisle Street. Likewise the third makes
  Boston Street into Breed Street and the fourth makes the existing Breed Street
  into Bird Street. If the clauses were applied in sequence rather than
  simultaneously the results would differ, and nothing in the ordinance says
  which is meant.
- The ground is Boyle Heights and the southern city edge — Hancock's survey
  blocks 59 and 60, Pine Street, Walnut Avenue, Washington Street. None of it is
  in the project's current `dtla` extract.
