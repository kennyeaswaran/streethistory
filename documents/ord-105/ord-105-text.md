ORDINANCE No. 105.

AN ORDINANCE dedicating certain lands as a public street and highway.

The Mayor and Council of the City of Los Angeles do ordain as follows:

SECTION 1. The following-described real property, situated in the City of Los
Angeles, County of Los Angeles, State of California, and more particularly
described as follows: lot (15) fifteen and southerly part of lot (17) seventeen,
block (35½) thirty-five and one-half, Ord's survey of the City of Los Angeles,
more particularly described as follows: commencing at the south-east corner of
said lot (15) fifteen, and running thence along south boundary of said lot (15)
fifteen westerly to the east boundary of Rosas street; thence along the east
boundary of Rosas street northerly (63) sixty-three feet to a point; thence on a
line parallel to the south boundary of said lot (15) fifteen, and distant at all
points (60) sixty feet therefrom, easterly to the

[p. 333]

north-west corner of said lot (15) fifteen; thence along the east boundary of
said lot (15) fifteen southerly to the point of beginning, is hereby and forever
dedicated to the use of the public, and declared a public street and highway.

SEC. 2. The name of the above-described tract of land shall be High street.

SEC. 3. The Clerk of the Council shall certify to the passage of this ordinance,
and shall cause the same to be published once in the Los Angeles Daily HERALD,
and thereupon and thereafter it shall take effect and be in force.

I hereby certify that the foregoing ordinance was adopted by the Council of the
City of Los Angeles at its meeting of November 17th, A. D. 1883.

                                        W. W. ROBINSON,
                    Clerk of the Council of the City of Los Angeles.

Approved this 19th day of November, A. D. 1883.

                                        C. E. THOM,
                                                    Mayor.

Published November 21st, 1883.

## Reading notes (not part of the text)

- The metes-and-bounds are internally odd as printed: the call runs east "to the
  **north-west** corner of said lot 15" and then south along lot 15's **east**
  boundary. One of the two is a slip in the original. The shape is nonetheless
  unambiguous — a strip **sixty feet wide** along the south edge of lot 15,
  running **east from Rosas street**.
- **Block 35½, Ord's Survey** is the ground of `documents/mr003-240`, the sheet
  that letters **Teed Street**, **Rosas Street**, **High St** and **Short St**.
