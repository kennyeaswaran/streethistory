ORDINANCE No. 103.

AN ORDINANCE naming a certain alley, Mott Alley.

The Mayor and Council of the City of Los Angeles do ordain as follows:

SECTION 1. That certain lane or alley in the City of Los Angeles, between Main
street and Spring street, and extending from First street to Second street, is
hereby named and the same shall be hereafter known as "Mott Alley."

SEC. 2. The Clerk of the Council shall certify to the passage of this ordinance,
and shall cause the same to be published once in the Los Angeles Daily HERALD,
and thereupon and thereafter it shall take effect and be in force.

I hereby certify that the foregoing ordinance was adopted by the Council of the
City of Los Angeles, at its meeting of October 15th, A. D. 1883.

                                        W. W. ROBINSON,
                    Clerk of the Council of the City of Los Angeles.

Approved this 16th day of October, A. D. 1883.

                                        C. E. THOM,
                                                    Mayor.

Published October 19th, 1883.

## Reading notes (not part of the text)

- The name is **quoted in the original**: `known as "Mott Alley."` — including
  the capital A.
- The extent is exact and modern: **between Main and Spring, from First to
  Second**.
- Two later resolutions in the same volume work on it: **Res. 183 (p. 309)**, of
  intention to improve and grade Mott alley, and **Res. 195 (p. 323)**, ordering
  the grading and improvement of Mott Alley.
