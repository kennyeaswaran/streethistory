## What is still missing

1. **`bath-street-plaza` is a stub in `names-new.js`**, minted in review from
   the 1873 plaza survey: no namesake, no sources, no categories. This
   ordinance now gives it an end date (Mar. 19, 1883) and an instrument, which
   is most of what a move into `names.js` needs. The obvious namesake question —
   whether the street was named for a bath house, the Bath of England, or a
   person — is unresearched.
2. **How far north Bath ran.** The ordinance does not say, and the 1873 survey
   only covers the plaza block. Robinson 1884 itself has more: Res. 194 (p. 323)
   and Res. 198 (p. 326) establish and improve the grade of Main street "from
   California street to Washington street", and Res. 88 (p. 160) fixes its lines
   between Washington and Jefferson — all south of the plaza, so they bound the
   problem from the other end.
3. **Read it with `documents/ord-76`**, adopted five days later, which names the
   short frontage in front of the plaza church UPPER MAIN STREET as far as
   Alameda. Whether Upper Main is a leftover of Bath, a part of it, or something
   else is the open question.
