Teed 1889 Vol. IV prints ordinances **318–432** and contains no omnibus
renaming; its three street renamings are Ords. 320, 345 and 381. The reason is
structural: the 1889 charter **restarted the numbering**, and the cull was
enacted as **Ordinance No. 48 (NEW Series), 22 April 1889** — which this project
already holds, as `documents/lah-1889-05-10`. Vol. IV is the last volume of the
old series; the cull is the new series' first big act. **Strike this from
IN-PERSON.md §1c and from the Law Library errand.**

⭐ **Note the series shape**: Caswell 1878 and Robinson 1884 index by SUBJECT
(street name), giving ordinance/resolution number and page. Teed 1887 lists by
ORDINANCE NUMBER with a title. So for a street name, search Caswell/Robinson;
for an ordinance, scan Teed.

## ✔ What the indexes already settle without pulling a page

### Two renaming chains, in full

- **Turner → Jackson (Ord. 81, 1884) → Sonora (Ord. 97, 1884)** — two changes in
  one year, both numbered.
- **Wasp → Yale (Caswell §394, by 1878)**, and Yale is still Yale in 1884
  (Robinson indexes "Yale street, grade at Virgin street").

### An extent for Virgin Street ⭐⭐

`documents/ord-1887-08-22` — *"An Ordinance changing the name of Virgin
Street"* — reads **"Ordinance No. —"** in the ledger, and we have never known
where Virgin ran. Robinson 1884 fixes it by its cross streets:

- grade **between Alameda and Yale streets** (Res. 135, p. 231)
- grade **at Reservoir street** (Res. 137, p. 233)
- improvement **between Upper Main and Yale streets** (Res. 141 p. 238; Res. 181 p. 298)
- sewer (Res. 145 p. 246; Res. 169 p. 280)
- and Res. 58: improvement of **Buena Vista street between Virgin and College**

So **Virgin ran across the Sonoratown/Chinatown grid north of the Plaza**,
between Upper Main and Yale, crossing Alameda, Reservoir and Buena Vista. That
is a placeable extent for a renaming we could not put on the ground.

### Short Street placed ⭐

Res. 56: *"Establishing grade of **Buena Vista street, between Short and College
streets**"*. So Short Street is a cross street of Buena Vista north of the
Plaza — and Teed Ord. 152 then folds it into **Bellevue avenue**. WANTED P2's
missing "Short" is answered in outline.

### Buena Vista and Macy confirmed in force

Both are HARD `names` items on the switchover list. Robinson 1884 indexes
**Buena Vista street** (Res. 56, 58; Temple street grade at Buena Vista, Res.
126/207; S.P.R.R. track grade at Buena Vista, Res. 90) and **Macy street**
(Res. 19, 26, 27 — grade from Alameda to the covered bridge). Teed 1887 Ord. 247
provides for the **widening of Buena Vista street**.

### ⭐ A NEGATIVE THAT SUPPORTS OUR BIGGEST INFERENCE

**There is no Wilson Street in either the 1878 or the 1884 index.** Caswell 1878
runs Washington → Wasp → Wilmington → Woolen Mills → Wolfskill → Workshops;
Robinson 1884 runs Washington → Water → Wilmington → Wood → Workman → Yale.
Neither has a Wilson. Consistent with `lemon-st-arts-district`'s reading that
**Lemon became Wilson in 1887** — and Teed 1887's three omnibus renamings
(§2 above) are where that change should be. ⚠ Absence from a subject index is
weak evidence: a street with no ordinance about it simply does not appear.

### ⚠ Charity Street is not yet resolved, and I over-claimed earlier

Robinson 1884 shows Charity as a cross street from **Second** (Res. 3, grade
between Second and Temple) down through Sixth, Seventh, Tenth, Twelfth; Teed
Ord. 156/188 establishes its lines **from 84 ft north of Pico to Jefferson**.
Then Teed **Ord. 286 changes "Charity street" to "Grand avenue"**.

But the **Violé 1904 map still letters a CHARITY ST** in the virtue cluster
(Faith, Joy, Bethel, Beulah) south of the old city boundary near Jefferson.
Two readings, and only Ord. 286's text decides:

1. Ord. 286 renamed the **whole** length, and Violé's Charity is a different,
   later street in that southern cluster; or
2. Ord. 286 renamed only the **downtown stretch**, leaving the southern end as
   Charity — i.e. a **qualified** change, which is what the row would need.

⚠ On 2026-09-18 I told Kenny flatly that these were two different streets. That
was premature — the index shows one Charity running from Second to Jefferson.
**Pull p. 248 and read the extent clause.**

## How to pull a page

In the Gale reader, the page images are downloadable a range at a time; the
citation page Gale appends carries the doc id. Name downloads
`<volume>-p<page>.pdf` and drop them in `inbox/compiled-ordinances/`.

⚠ **UCLA network only** (`u=uclosangeles`). ⭐ **Check whether UCI licenses
MMLP** — `https://libguides.law.uci.edu/az/databases`, or refdesk@law.uci.edu.
If it does, all of this is doable from Irvine, and the Law Library errand in
IN-PERSON.md §1c shrinks to the 1889 Vol. IV alone.
