## Dunigan Tract — closed, and the reason is the finding

Carleton and Summerfield's Sub of the Dunigan Tract has **no Map-Ref obtainable
from NavigateLA, and never will**. Every parcel the assessor holds in that tract
is DELETED, struck off the roll between September 1987 and March 1988:

    5134004003  1317 GEORGIA ST   deleted 09/24/1987
    5134004006  1355 GEORGIA ST   deleted 11/17/1987
    5134004009  1407 GEORGIA ST   deleted 03/28/1988
    5134006003  1358 GEORGIA ST   deleted 03/28/1988

That is the Convention Center expansion eating the block. NavigateLA reports a
Map-Ref by looking up a live parcel, so with no live parcel in the tract the
lookup has nothing to answer with — which is why the searches came back empty
rather than wrong. The addresses geocode to nothing: 1317 and 1355 Georgia St
both snap back to 1099 Georgia St, the last addressed parcel on the street.

Two consequences for the model. First, the `nevada-1886` stretch of Georgia
Street is **pavement that no longer exists**, so it can never be confirmed by a
modern parcel and its extent has to come from the plats and directories alone.
Second, the Huntington has no Dunigan, Carleton or Summerfield map either
(checked 2026-09-06), so the recorded sheet would have to be found by walking
Misc Records pages or through a title-company index.

The assessor's deleted-parcel records are themselves a source worth remembering:
they preserve the tract name and lot numbers for ground that has been erased,
and `api/search/legal` returns them alongside live parcels without flagging
which is which. Check `ParcelStatus` via `api/parceldetail?ain=` before
concluding a tract is still on the ground.

## How the Map-Refs were obtained

Two shortcuts, now written up in TRACT-RESEARCH.md:

    https://portal.assessor.lacounty.gov/api/search/legal?legaldesc=BELL+ADDITION
    https://navigatela.lacity.org/navigatela/reports/nlaby_pin.cfm?pin=124-5A205-29

The first is tract name -> AINs and addresses, no browser splash. The second is
the Parcel Description Report as a plain URL, once you have the PIN.
