## Method note — corridors, not areas

First attempt used area polygons (a 560 m band west of Figueroa, an 800 m box
over Crown Hill). That returned 648 and 592 parcels and **45 new sheets** — far
more than the job needs, because a band catches every filing in every block it
crosses, most of which never touch the grey street.

Re-running as **one ±80 m corridor per grey street segment** cut it to 37 and
made the result attributable: each sheet below is listed with the corridors it
actually fronts and how many parcels it holds on each. Worth doing this way
whenever the target is a street rather than a district.

Also worth noting for the next sweep: **the corpus has grown from 284 folders to
363 since 2026-09-15**, and 15 of the sheets this sweep found were already in
it. Check `documents/` before listing — and match on a prefix, because the
folder may be `mr003-046-p1`, `tr0015-166a-p2` or a parked `_mr005-400`.

## Three holes no map will close

1. **⚠ Olympic east of Central Avenue is a geometry gap, not a document gap.**
   check-legacy reports "no generated segment overlaps this ground", and the
   reason is that **the modern street is not in `data/streets-geometry.js` at all**:
   the easternmost mapped Olympic node is lon −118.24453, lat **34.03234** —
   at Central, on the neighbourhood's own southern edge. The Overpass bbox is
   `s: 34.033`, and Olympic drops below it immediately east of Central. No sheet
   can turn that segment blue until the bbox is extended south. This is the same
   edge that was flagged on 2026-09-14 for the Reyes Tract placement, so the two
   want fixing together.
2. **The grey on 2nd and 5th runs off the west edge.** The pieces labelled
   "west of Lake", "Westlake to Bonnie Brae" and "west of Burlington" sit at
   lon −118.278 to −118.282, west of the neighbourhood's `w: -118.272`. This
   sweep covers everything from Union eastward; those pieces need the bbox
   widened, not more sheets.
3. **Two `DM` references have no constructible URL** and between them hold 53
   parcels on the 3rd Street corridor: `DM 42-409/410`, Subdivision of Lots
   500–503 of the Reservoir Lands (the **Woolen Mill** tract — already flagged
   on the 09-15 Boylston list), and `DM 569-52`, Northerly portion of the
   **Satter Tract**. `L S 1-65`, Blanchard's Re-subdivision of Block 5 Woolen
   Mill Tract, is a third series with no URL. The Woolen Mill names recur across
   both sweeps now, so that series is worth solving once.
